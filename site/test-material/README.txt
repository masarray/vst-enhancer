ArSonKuPik Global Discovery P2 test material

This folder contains reproducibility aids for audio-engineering evaluation:

- measurement-log-template.csv — records signal path, software version, routing, controls, levels and measurement notes.
- ab-listening-log-template.csv — records repeatable A/B listening observations without implying universal preference.
- test-signal-manifest.json — defines the browser-generated WAV signals and their safety/level semantics.

The WAV generator lives at https://arsonkupik.pages.dev/audio-test-signals/ and runs locally in the browser. Generated signal level is a digital peak ceiling, not calibrated SPL, LUFS or RMS.

Start with monitor volume low. High-frequency or continuous test signals can be uncomfortable. The material is not intended for hearing tests or medical diagnosis.
