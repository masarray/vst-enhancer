1 kHz sine

Reference level and basic signal-path checks

  • Useful for confirming gain, routing, meter response and channel consistency.
  • The selected dBFS value represents digital peak amplitude.
  • Do not infer acoustic SPL without a separately calibrated monitoring chain.
Log sweep

Broad frequency-path inspection

  • Sweeps from 20 Hz to the lower of 20 kHz or 45% of sample rate.
  • Useful for inspecting filtering, resonances or frequency-dependent behaviour.
  • A sweep alone does not describe transient, distortion or perceived spatial quality.
Pink noise

Repeatable broadband noise

  • Uses a deterministic seeded approximation so repeated generation is consistent for the same settings.
  • Normalized to the selected peak ceiling, not a calibrated loudness target.
  • Useful for spectral-path checks and repeatable broadband excitation.
Stereo checks

Channel identity and polarity behaviour

  • Channel sequence: both, left, right, both.
  • Polarity sequence: first half in phase, second half with the right channel inverted.
  • Use low playback level; polarity cancellation depends on the playback and summing path.
